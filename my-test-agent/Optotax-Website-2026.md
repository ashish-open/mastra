## Key Features

### GST Filing & Compliance

- File GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9, and other GST returns directly from Optotax
- Handles returns for unlimited GSTINs under a single account
- Suitable for Chartered Accountants (CAs), tax practitioners, advocates, and articles managing multiple clients

### Reconciliation

- Automated reconciliation between purchase register and GSTR-2B
- Match invoices against counterparty-filed data
- Identify mismatches, missing invoices, and ITC discrepancies
- Export reconciliation reports in standard formats

### OptoAI

- AI-powered assistance built into the platform
- Available on all plans including Pay as you go
- Helps with filing guidance, error detection, and compliance queries

### Client Management

- Manage multiple clients/GSTINs from a single dashboard
- Add unlimited clients on all plans
- Invite team members with role-based access

### Notice Management (Enterprise only)

- Track and respond to GST notices within the platform
- Centralized notice inbox linked to GSTINs
- Status tracking for open, pending, and resolved notices

### Litigation Management (Enterprise only)

- Manage GST litigation cases
- Track hearings, submissions, and outcomes
- Document management for case files

### Challan Management

- Create and track GST payment challans
- Record tax liability payments
- Match challans against filed returns

### Reports

- Download detailed GST reports including GSTR-1, GSTR-3B summaries, reconciliation reports, and audit trail exports
- Report downloads are the unit of consumption for billing (not filings — filings are always unlimited)
- Reports available: GSTR-1 report, GSTR-3B report, GSTR-9 report, reconciliation report, client summary reports

## Common Questions

**Is Optotax free?**
No. Optotax previously offered a free plan but has since introduced paid pricing. GST *filings* are unlimited on all plans, but downloading reports costs credits. The Pay as you go plan charges ₹25 per report download with no monthly commitment.

**What counts as a "report"?**
A report download (PDF or Excel export of GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, reconciliation, etc.) consumes one report credit. Filing a return does NOT consume a report credit — filings are always unlimited.

**Can I add multiple team members?**
Yes. All plans including Pay as you go support unlimited users/seats at no extra cost.

**Is there a free trial?**
Contact Optotax sales or check the website for current trial availability.

**Who is Optotax for?**
Primarily for CAs, tax practitioners, advocates, and article assistants managing GST compliance for multiple clients. Individual businesses can also use it.

**What GST returns can I file?**
GSTR-1 (outward supplies), GSTR-3B (monthly summary), GSTR-9 (annual return), GSTR-9C (reconciliation statement), and other applicable returns.
