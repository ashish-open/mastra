**FAQ: Optotax**

* ## **What is Optotax?**

Optotax is a GST returns filing software through which you can file GST R1, R-3B, and R9 returns.

* ## **Is Optotax free software?**

At this moment, Optotax is a free-to-use platform for all tax professionals.

* ## **Is Optotax supporting both Regular and Composition schemes?**

No, Optotax supports only regular-type schemes.

* ## **Can I change from a Regular to a Composition scheme?**

No, you cannot change the scheme from regular to composition in Optotax. Optotax only supports the Regular scheme.

* ## **How to file GSTR-1 in Optotax?**

To file GST R1 in Optotax, follow the steps as mentioned below:

1. Go to the My Client tab under the Home section and select the client for whom you want to file GST.

4. From the dropdown under GSTR-1/IFF, choose Monthly or Quarterly as applicable.

* ## **What are the different types of GST returns I can file in Optotax?**

There are several types of GST returns based on your business activities and turnover:

* **GSTR-1:** Details of outward supplies (monthly or quarterly).   
* **GSTR-3B:** Monthly / Quarterly summary of inward and outward supplies, including tax paid.  
* **GSTR-9:** Annual return which summarizes your business’s GST activities for the Calendar year.

* ## **I am unable to download the reports and I have 500+ invoices?**

2. Click on the View Profile / Manage Profile and then click on Manage API Access and activate the access for 30 days. 

Post this, return to the Optotax dashboard and try downloading the reports with OTP.

* ## **What should I do if my summary is not being fetched during GST filing?**

If you are facing issues with fetching the summary of your GST returns, here are a few steps to resolve the problem:

* **Check Data Entry:** Verify that all the required details and data (invoices, sales/purchases) are entered correctly in the Excel or JSON.  
* **Clear Cache & Cookies:** Sometimes, old data stored in the browser cache can cause issues. Clear your browser cache and cookies, and try fetching the summary again.  
* **Check Internet Connectivity:** A slow or interrupted internet connection may prevent the system from fetching the summary. Ensure you have a stable and fast internet connection.  
* **System Overload:** If the GST portal or our platform is experiencing heavy traffic, the summary may take longer to load. Try waiting for a while and refresh the page.  
* **Ensure Data Accuracy:** Make sure that no pending errors or validation issues exist in your GST filings. Check for any incomplete or incorrect entries.  
* **Browser Settings:** Disable any browser extensions that may be blocking the request or try using an incognito window for a clean session.

If the issue persists, please reach out to our Customer support team via one or all of the following methods:

* **Email: [support@optotax.com](mailto:support@optotax.com)**  
* **Call the helpline number \- 8103810350**  
* **Live chat from the Optotax dashboard**

The team will check your account and guide you through the process.

* ## **Why are my GST returns frozen and unable to do the next filing step?**

This error can occur for various reasons as follows:

* **Incomplete Filing:** If any required fields are missing or incomplete in your return, the system may not be able to generate the summary.  
* **GST Portal Downtime:** Sometimes, the GST portal experiences downtime or technical issues.   
* **GSTN Error:** The issue might be from the GST network (GSTN) side. You can wait and try again later or check for updates from the GST portal.
